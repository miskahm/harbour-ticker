## Data providers

Quotes come from third-party endpoints: Yahoo Finance `v8/finance/chart`
(unofficial, keyless), the opennordicstocks.net CDN (no key), and Finnhub
(optional, requires a free API key). These are data-provider services and are
not part of this application's source licensing.
